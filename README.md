## 🎓 Master's Thesis

### *Revisiting the Conditions of the Boltzmann Equation via the Classical Correlation Function*
**M.Sc. in Condensed Matter Physics** | *Materials and Energy Research Center (MERC)*

A theoretical and analytical study investigating the role of statistical correlations in the reduced description of many-particle systems. 
- **Core Focus:** Analyzed the Boltzmann equation and the BBGKY hierarchy to understand the transition from microscopic reversible dynamics to effective kinetic descriptions.
- **Key Findings:** Demonstrated how the explicit elimination of two-particle correlation information generates memory effects in the reduced dynamics, and formulated the precise conditions required for the Markovian limit.
- **Thermodynamic Implications:** Examined the connection between collision dynamics, the linearized operator near equilibrium, the $H$-function, and effective irreversibility, maintaining a strict distinction between structural dynamics and phenomenological approximations.

## 🚀 Selected Computational Project

### [Quantum Entropy Scaling](https://github.com/AbolfazlAmiri1998/QuantumEntropyScaling)
*A computational study of subsystem entanglement dynamics in finite transverse-field Ising chains.*

This project investigates how the temporal behavior of single-qubit von Neumann entropy scales with system size, emphasizing finite-size effects and the theoretical limits of small quantum systems.
- **Methods:** Exact diagonalization, quantum time evolution, and partial trace operations.
- **Analysis:** Evaluated entanglement entropy dynamics, time-averaged entropy, and operational recurrence limits.
- **Engineering:** Built with Python, featuring automated testing (Pytest), robust data visualization, and full reproducibility.
